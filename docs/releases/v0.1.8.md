# DevNook v0.1.8

全局服务管理页现在把运行中的服务放在上方，方便快速查看当前状态和端口。

## 更新内容

- 已停止和未启动的配置收进默认折叠的「可启动」区。展开后可启动服务、查看启动命令和历史日志，搜索命中时会自动展开。
- 停止当前选中的服务后，右侧仍可查看它的日志。失败、阻断和身份待核验的配置保留在「需要关注」区。

## 安装与升级

按芯片选择 Apple Silicon 或 Intel Mac 的 DMG，打开后将 DevNook 拖入 Applications。两个包均支持 macOS 12 或更高版本，采用 **ad-hoc 签名，尚未通过 Apple 公证**。首次打开可能被 macOS 拦截，安装前请核对下载来源和下方的校验和。

## English

- The global Services page shows running services first. Stopped and never-started configurations are available in the collapsed **Available to start** section. Search expands the section when a configuration matches.
- You can start a service or read its past run logs from that section. When the selected service stops, its logs stay open. Failed, blocked, and unverified services remain visible under **Needs attention**.

Choose the Apple Silicon or Intel DMG for macOS 12 or later. Open the DMG and drag DevNook into Applications. Both packages are **ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple**. macOS may block the first launch. Verify the download source and checksum before installing.

## 校验 / Verify

将 DMG 和对应的校验文件放在同一目录。Place the DMG and its checksum file in the same directory.

```sh
# Apple Silicon
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
# Intel Mac
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS-x86_64
```
